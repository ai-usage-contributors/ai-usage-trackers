SYNTHETIC QUOTA REPORTS - reviewed 2026-11-11

Claude and Antigravity support is experimental. These fixtures and passing tests
support theoretical implementations only. Contributors with subscriptions must
validate real usage/capture and quota behavior before verified support can be
claimed; see TECHNICAL.md section 5.

0.18.0 integration cases are also authored inline in ProviderIntegrationTest.kt.
Those synthetic Claude assistant records follow the message/usage transcript
contract exposed by the official hooks documentation and token category rules;
Antigravity captures follow documented stream-json DONE step usage. No prompt or
real subscriber data is copied. Cache/thinking fields deliberately overlap totals.
https://code.claude.com/docs/en/hooks
https://antigravity.google/docs/cli/headless/
The latter explicitly describes per-step usage separately from cumulative result
usage; tests exclude ACTIVE and result records. Helper arrival time supplies the
step completion timestamp, not a fabricated provider timestamp.

https://github.com/harbor-framework/harbor/issues/2685
Versioned Antigravity CLI transcript incompatibility report, acknowledged/closed
by a repository collaborator, reinforces that native step logs differ from old
Gemini logs. Subscription ownership is not established here; supplementary only.
Native step-log parsing is deliberately not admitted on that evidence alone.

All numeric values and dates in these fixtures are synthetic. These files are
not subscribers' private logs and contain no account identities or credentials.

Authoritative field contracts:
https://code.claude.com/docs/en/statusline
rate_limits.five_hour / seven_day: used_percentage (0..100), resets_at (epoch).
https://antigravity.google/docs/cli/statusline/
quota map: remaining_fraction or remaining_amount, reset_time and disabled.
The context_window fields deliberately disagree with quota to detect accidental
use of context occupancy as subscription entitlement.

reproductions. Further reports identify Team seats and newer CLI versions. Strong

quota and freshness separate; independent subscription ownership is not verified.

Versioned claude.ai subscription/ACP report corroborates per-window rather than

These undocumented SDK shapes are not accepted as documented status-line input.
https://github.com/pingdotgg/t3code/issues/16104
Versioned CLI observations and maintainer-linked fixes corroborate separate
Gemini and Claude/GPT pools, five-hour/weekly distinctions and account-attribution
risks. This report alone does not verify subscription ownership; use its shapes
only where the official contract independently agrees. Do not implement its
private endpoints, proxy or model-probe approaches.
